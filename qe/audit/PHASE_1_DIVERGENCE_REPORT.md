## 1. Failures first

1. **No concept has two implementations that agree.** 20 of 24 concepts are implemented in two or more places, each time differently. BOS, order block, and breaker each have one implementation. SMT and iFVG have none.
2. **"Sweep" means four different things:**
   - AI detector: a bar within 0.5% *below* the level.
   - Fractal: any undercut of the previous candle.
   - `algo_detection`: 0.5% tolerance plus a volume spike.
   - Research: a new pivot low below the prior pivot low, by any amount.

   None has a minimum penetration in ticks.
3. **"FVG" is always a two-bar gap.** Three different two-bar variants exist and none is the common three-candle imbalance.
4. **Stops are not structural in 3 of the 6 implementations that define a stop.** The fractal validator and the Smart Blend Pine script move the stop 30% toward entry. Their comment justifies this with a "+$1893" gain from the synthetic backtests (D-030). UT Bot uses an ATR trailing stop.
5. **R:R rules are golden-ratio folklore, and in Pine they never bind.** At least seven implementations use 1.618 as a target or minimum. God's Plan requires net R ≥ 2 after costs.
   - In Smart Blend, R:R is a constant 2.94 of the level geometry (D-046).
   - In UT Bot, it equals 1.618 up to float rounding, so about half of otherwise-valid signals are rejected by rounding noise in a float64 replay (D-045; confirm in TradingView).
6. **Entry timing differs by implementation:**
   - Research: next bar open (sound).
   - Fractal: close of the last candle supplied, which may still be forming.
   - Fib validators: a limit fill at 0.618 is simply assumed.
   - Pine: market entry on the signal bar while R:R is computed from the 0.618 level, not the fill.
7. **One-attempt exists nowhere.** The only duplicate guard is keyed on an id that is always empty (D-003).

## 2. What is worth keeping

The `QuantumEdge/research` fib family is the only implementation with explicit timing discipline:

- confirmed pivots with `discovery_bar`;
- sweep, then reclaim within N bars;
- displacement measured in ATR and % terms;
- a retest zone with expiry;
- configurable confirmation triggers;
- next-open entry, with the stop checked before the target.

Its concepts map onto most of the God's Plan chain. It is the recommended **reference** for Phase 2's candidate definitions. The GP spec still decides each definition; the fib code is not the authority.

**Prior evidence to carry into Phase 2 (L0, not reproducible):** fib6 tested a one-hour "reclaim after sweep" trigger on ETFs and recorded **−0.201R, 30.6% win rate**, then rejected it (`QuantumEdge/research/fib7/live_spec.py:62-65`). This is the closest thing in the repo to a God's Plan test. It is negative, but it can't be reproduced (D-035), and it was on ETFs, not futures.
